# SEO Audit and Ranking Plan

Audit date: 2026-09-05

Site: https://mukul-kumar-mishra.github.io/

## Executive Verdict

The site is technically crawlable, but it is not yet positioned for fast, durable ranking growth. The strongest asset is the set of long-form system-design articles. The largest weaknesses are thin or JavaScript-only index pages, inconsistent metadata, visibly broken copy spacing, weak internal linking between articles, and limited evidence of authority outside the site.

Current priority: make every important URL useful and trustworthy in the initial HTML, then earn relevant links and mentions. Do not spend time on keyword stuffing or more visual redesign.

## What Was Checked

- Live homepage, system-design index, representative article, `robots.txt`, and `sitemap.xml`.
- Local HTML, article catalog, metadata, canonical URLs, headings, structured data, image alt text, and internal links.
- Live HTTP status and content types. The checked URLs returned `200` responses.

## Strengths

- HTTPS GitHub Pages deployment is working.
- `robots.txt` allows crawling and points to the sitemap.
- The sitemap includes the main pages and article URLs.
- Canonical URLs are present on most index and article pages.
- Most long-form articles have a unique title, description, article image, author, and `TechArticle` JSON-LD.
- Article URLs are readable and topic-specific.
- The homepage has a useful `Person` entity with GitHub, LinkedIn, and Dev.to references.

## Highest-Impact Problems

### P0: Repair the system-design index's initial HTML

`system-design/index.html` has an empty `#articleList` in the source (lines 67-75). Article cards are generated by JavaScript from `articles.json` (lines 107-213). Search engines can render JavaScript, but relying on rendering makes discovery, indexing, snippets, and resilience weaker than static links.

Fix by server-rendering all article cards as normal `<article>` elements with crawlable `<a>` links. Keep JavaScript only for search, filtering, and progressive enhancement. The home page should also expose links to every important article, not only a small featured subset.

### P0: Fix the metadata and schema gap on the cell-based article

`system-design/cell-based-control-plane.html` has a canonical and title, but no meta description, author, Open Graph description/image, Twitter metadata, or article JSON-LD. It is in the sitemap, so this is an avoidable quality gap.

Add a unique description, `TechArticle` JSON-LD, author, `datePublished`, `dateModified`, `headline`, `image`, and `mainEntityOfPage`. Add a visible author/date line if the article is intended to compete with the other posts.

### P0: Correct all missing spaces and factual copy defects

The deployed copy repeatedly contains joins such as `pipelinesand`, `Kafkaand`, `mathand`, `costand`, and `observabilityand`. Examples are in `index.html:6`, `index.html:87-90`, `system-design/index.html:6`, and several article descriptions and paragraphs.

Run a content pass across every HTML page and `articles.json`. These errors reduce readability, look automated, and can reduce trust and engagement. Also check claims, dates, quoted material, and source links before publishing; sensational or unsupported claims are a serious trust risk even when technical SEO is correct.

### P1: Strengthen internal linking and topical clusters

Every article should link to:

- The system-design hub.
- Two or three closely related articles using descriptive anchor text.
- The author's profile or an author page.
- A relevant source section where claims depend on external reporting.

The current links are mostly navigation and occasional inline references. Add a consistent "Related system designs" block near the end of each article. Link hub pages to every article in static HTML.

### P1: Make article metadata internally consistent

Several article cards use a click-oriented headline while the `<title>` and JSON-LD use a different headline. That is acceptable when intentional, but the primary topic and entity should remain obvious in all three places. Use this pattern:

`[Topic] system design: [specific outcome or problem] | Mukul Kumar Mishra`

Keep descriptions around 140-165 characters, state what the reader will learn, and remove `meta keywords`; they do not help Google rankings and add maintenance noise.

### P1: Add a real author and editorial trust layer

Add an `/about` or `about.html` page with a concise career history, location, skills, contact links, and a clear explanation of which work is firsthand experience versus modeled analysis. Link it from every article.

For each post, show author, publication date, last updated date, scope/methodology, and sources. For cost estimates, label assumptions directly beside the estimate. Avoid implying private access when the analysis is reconstructed from public sources.

### P1: Measure indexing and query performance

In Google Search Console:

1. Verify the domain/property.
2. Submit `https://mukul-kumar-mishra.github.io/sitemap.xml`.
3. Inspect and request indexing for the homepage, hub, and five strongest articles.
4. Check Page indexing for crawled-but-not-indexed and duplicate/canonical issues.
5. Record queries, impressions, clicks, and average position weekly.

Do not repeatedly request indexing for every small edit. Publish a meaningful update, then inspect the affected URLs.

- Every important page returns `200` and has one canonical URL.
- Every page has exactly one meaningful H1.
- No important content exists only behind JavaScript, a click, or a search box.
- Article title, H1, description, OG title, and JSON-LD describe the same topic.
- All local links and image paths resolve.

## Ranking Reality

There is no reliable shortcut to top rankings. Technical fixes can improve crawlability and click-through rate quickly, but competitive queries require time, useful original content, links, and demonstrated expertise. The fastest legitimate path for this site is to own a narrow cluster around practical system design and AI infrastructure cost analysis, publish consistently, and promote each substantial article to an audience that can cite it.

- 100% of sitemap URLs indexed or with a documented reason not to index.
- Zero missing metadata/schema gaps on priority pages.
- Zero malformed word joins in visible or metadata copy.
- At least one crawlable internal link path from the homepage to every article.
- Growing impressions for the mapped queries within 4-8 weeks.
