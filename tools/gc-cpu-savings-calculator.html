GC CPU Savings Calculator

Collector burn is fleet spend times GC share. The defaults model a $100,000 fleet at 25% GC share with a 30% overall cut: $30,000 a month back.

Every result is pre-rendered with the default inputs, so the numbers are readable with JavaScript off. Results update as you type and nothing leaves your browser. Part of the Buildopsy engineering calculators.

Model limits: Savings equal fleet spend times the overall CPU cut you enter — the GC shares diagnose the cause but do not set the savings. The 25%-to-2% GC band and 30% cut default reproduce the reported shape of one company's rollout, not a promise about yours. Enter your invoiced fleet spend and measured GC share. GC profiling comes from your runtime metrics, not from this page.
