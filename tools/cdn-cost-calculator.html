CDN pricing models

CDN bills generally follow usage-based pricing or a flat-rate/committed plan. This calculator models the first shape; the reference table shows why a single per-GB number does not fit every provider.

ModelExampleHow it bills
Usage-basedAWS CloudFront pay-as-you-go, US/EuropeThe first 1 TB each month is free. The next 9 TB is $0.085/GB, the next 40 TB is $0.080/GB and the next 100 TB is $0.060/GB. HTTP requests are $0.0075 per 10,000 and HTTPS requests are $0.01 per 10,000 after the first 10 million requests each month.
Flat-rate planAWS CloudFront plansFree $0, Pro $15, Business $200 and Premium $1,000 per distribution per month, each with a usage allowance and no overage charges.
Plan or commitmentCloudflare, FastlyPriced by plan or committed volume. A per-GB model does not map directly; check the provider's current pricing.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the monthly CDN estimate calculated?

The model adds monthly bandwidth times its entered price per GB to requests in groups of 10,000 times the entered request price. With the example inputs, the estimate is $925 before plan fees or free-tier allowances.

Is bandwidth or request volume the bigger cost in this example?

Bandwidth is about 92 percent of this example bill and requests about 8 percent. The mix changes when traffic volume, object size or the unit rates change.

Does this include CDN free tiers or plan fees?

No. Enter billable bandwidth and requests after any applicable free allowance. Add fixed plan fees or other line items separately; rates differ by provider, region, protocol and plan.

How much does a CDN cost per GB?

It depends on the pricing model. CloudFront pay-as-you-go US/Europe charges $0.085 per GB after the 1 TB monthly free allowance, covering the next 9 TB before the $0.080 per GB tier. Flat-rate plans and committed contracts do not map to a single per-GB rate. Use the calculator with your contracted rate.
