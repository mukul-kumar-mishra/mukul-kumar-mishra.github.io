Billing realism checks

Two things decide a real CDN bill. Transfer is priced in volume tiers, so the rate falls as volume rises. And every cache miss pulls the object from your origin, which may bill that transfer again. CloudFront does not charge for cacheable data pulled from an AWS origin such as S3, so the origin rate starts at 0. Both are neutral at the defaults: 10,000 GB sits inside the first tier, and a 100% hit ratio pulls nothing.

At 100 TB delivered, tiered bandwidth comes to $7,219 a month against $8,704 at a flat $0.085. A 90% hit ratio on that volume still pulls 10,240 GB from origin. If your origin bills egress at $0.09/GB, that adds about $922; behind CloudFront, an AWS origin is not charged for it.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the monthly CDN estimate calculated?

The model adds monthly bandwidth across the provider's volume tiers to requests in groups of 10,000 times the entered request price, then adds origin egress for the share of traffic that misses the cache. With the example inputs, the estimate is $950 before plan fees or free-tier allowances.

Is bandwidth or request volume the bigger cost in this example?

Bandwidth is about 89 percent of this example bill and requests about 11 percent. The mix changes when traffic volume, object size, the unit rates or the cache hit ratio change.

Does this include CDN free tiers or plan fees?

No. Enter billable bandwidth and requests after any applicable free allowance. Add fixed plan fees or other line items separately; rates differ by provider, region, protocol and plan.

Does a cache miss cost extra?

It depends on the origin. A cache miss pulls the object from your origin, and that transfer may be billed by whoever hosts the origin rather than by the CDN. CloudFront does not charge for cacheable data pulled from an AWS origin such as S3. At 100 TB delivered and a 90 percent hit ratio, 10,240 GB still leaves origin; at $0.09 per GB that is about $922, and an origin that bills nothing adds nothing.

How much does a CDN cost per GB?

It depends on the pricing model. CloudFront pay-as-you-go US/Europe charges $0.085 per GB after the 1 TB monthly free allowance, covering the next 9 TB before the $0.080 per GB tier. Flat-rate plans and committed contracts do not map to a single per-GB rate. Use the calculator with your contracted rate.
