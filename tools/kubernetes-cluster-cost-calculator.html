Kubernetes Cluster Cost Calculator

A cluster bill is worker nodes plus control-plane fees plus persistent volumes. This example prices six nodes at $140 each, a gross $73 EKS control-plane fee and 500 GB of storage at $0.10/GB-month, for $963 before credits. GKE's credit can offset one eligible cluster.

Every result is pre-rendered with the default inputs, so the numbers are readable with JavaScript off. Results update as you type and nothing leaves your browser. Part of the Buildopsy engineering calculators.

Pricing note: This estimate totals worker compute, control-plane management and entered persistent storage only; it excludes networking, load balancers, logging and other services. Presets model EKS standard support ($0.10/hour), GKE gross management fees ($0.10/hour, before its credit), or AKS Free, Standard ($0.10/hour) and Premium ($0.60/hour) tiers. The presets change only the control-plane fee; node and storage rates stay as editable example inputs. The GKE $74.40 monthly credit covers one eligible zonal Standard or Autopilot cluster, not regional clusters; GKE Enterprise is exempt from the management fee. EKS extended support costs more.
