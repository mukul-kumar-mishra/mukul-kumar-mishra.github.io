Formula and assumptions

For n identical independent replicas with per-replica availability p, majority quorum is q = floor(n / 2) + 1. Quorum availability is Σ C(n,k) pᵏ (1−p)ⁿ⁻ᵏ for k = q…n.

This is a mathematical model, not a service-level prediction. Replica failures are rarely fully independent: shared zones, networks, control planes, software releases, and dependencies can dominate. It also excludes recovery time and quorum protocol behavior. Use fault-domain design and observed failure data in addition to this estimate.

Frequently asked questions

How is quorum availability calculated?

The calculator sums the probability of all combinations with at least a strict majority of available replicas, assuming the same independent availability for each replica.

Does replica count alone guarantee high availability?

No. Independence is a simplifying assumption; correlated failures, shared dependencies, and recovery behavior must be assessed separately.
