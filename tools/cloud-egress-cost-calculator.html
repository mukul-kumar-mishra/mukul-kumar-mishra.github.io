Cloud Egress Cost Calculator

Outbound transfer is the line item that surprises teams. This example prices 10,000 GB of AWS internet egress at the US East first-tier rate of $0.09 per GB with the full 100 GB monthly allowance remaining, for $891. Rates vary by region, destination and tier.

Every result is pre-rendered with the default inputs, so the numbers are readable with JavaScript off. Results update as you type and nothing leaves your browser. Part of the Buildopsy engineering calculators.

Model limits: The price and allowance are inputs for one transfer path. AWS's first 100 GB allowance is shared across eligible services and regions, so enter only the remaining amount. This model does not handle tiered rates or product-specific exceptions. See AWS transfer pricing and Google Cloud network pricing.
