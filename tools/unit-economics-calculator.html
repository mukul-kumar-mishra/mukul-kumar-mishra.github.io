Formula and limits

Monthly spend = fixed monthly spend + monthly logical requests × (1 + additional retries per request) × cost per attempt. Cost per million successful requests = monthly spend ÷ (monthly logical requests × end-to-end success rate) × 1,000,000.

The model assumes every attempt has the same cost, the retry average applies across the workload, and the entered success rate describes final logical outcomes. It uses a 30-day month. It does not model traffic peaks, different retry costs, revenue impact, provider discounts, or correlated failures. Use measured telemetry and current rates; this is an estimate, not a provider quote.
