Vector Database Cost Calculator

This scenario estimates float32 vector storage plus a serving-pod fleet. Ten million 1,536-dimension vectors with 2x index overhead and three $70 pods model to about $224 a month. Change the assumed rates to match your setup.

Every result is pre-rendered with the default inputs, so the numbers are readable with JavaScript off. Results update as you type and nothing leaves your browser. Part of the Buildopsy engineering calculators.

Model limits: Raw storage assumes four bytes per dimension (float32) and decimal GB (1 GB = 1 billion bytes), then applies the chosen index-overhead multiplier. This includes storage plus serving pods only. Query charges, backups, replicas and managed per-vector pricing are not included.
