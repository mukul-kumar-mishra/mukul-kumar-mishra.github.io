About the site

Buildopsy examines distributed systems, AI infrastructure, data pipelines, APIs, and cloud economics. Case studies use public disclosures and credible reporting, then connect architecture choices to explicit workload and cost assumptions readers can rerun in the calculators.

Modeled figures are estimates, not private invoices. Analysis distinguishes disclosed facts from inference. Commercial work does not buy editorial conclusions.

Editorial policy

Sources are linked, assumptions are stated, and estimates are labeled. Calculators run locally without sending their inputs to Buildopsy. Public articles, courses, and tools remain free. Desk names such as Rook, Ira, Marta, and Kabir are editorial labels, not individual contributors or claims of personal experience. Mukul Kumar Mishra is the named editor and accountable human author. See pricing for the service model.
