Correction · October 10, 2026

Shared AI Outage Cause Not Established

Buildopsy has withdrawn the previous version of this article. It presented reports of provider-specific service issues around September 3, 2026 as evidence of a common infrastructure failure, and asserted shared Azure and NVIDIA dependencies across providers.

The sources cited in that version did not establish a common cause or substantiate those dependency claims. Temporal overlap alone is not evidence that independent services share a failure domain. The probability and financial-loss calculations also relied on assumptions that were not supported by published incident data.

We removed the analysis from search and internal article listings. It should not be relied on as a postmortem. We will publish a replacement only if primary incident records support the claimed timeline and technical connection.

For sourced examples of regional failure propagation, see the AWS EU-SOUTH-2 packet-loss analysis and the DigitalOcean BLR1 upstream disruption analysis.
